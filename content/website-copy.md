## 2. The idea (white)

Headline: You want passionate, mind-blowing, leaves-you-a-little-altered intimate life and sex. Who doesn't? (italic: Who doesn't?)

Here's what usually happens instead.

In the beginning, it's easy. The butterfly phase carries you. You can't keep your hands off each other and everything feels new, because it is. It might not even feel like your absolute fuckyes but you’re willing to look past that because… well, the butterflies.

Then life settles. And somewhere along the way, your sex life settles too. Same order, same moves, same outcome. It's a little bit beige and you don’t quite know what to do about it.

(Single? You know this one from the inside. In your head, a little bit performing, low on your sex appeal confidence, low on ideas. Where's your body in all this? Where's the juice?)

You're stuck in a pattern that no longer turns you on. I call it the flatness and it's what I see most, by far.

So people reach for more. More intensity. Opening relationships. The next kink party. Fun, sometimes. And then the flatness comes back, because it was never about needing more.

What changes it is depth and imagination. Discovering that the small world you already have can hold a whole universe of eroticism. Same bed, same body, maybe even the same person you’re with and suddenly there's a door at the back you've never opened. The tiny world of your sheets, turned into the Narnia of delights.

And it doesn't stay in the bedroom. It becomes a current that's alive in how you move, eat, flirt, look at people and live out your purpose.

That kind of sex rarely just happens. It takes a little know-how. Knowledge and skill, basically. Both can be learned.

Connecting Heart and Sex is one way there. It's the one I've proven to work with 2,000 people who have been in my spaces. And it's how I live my own best sex life. That's why I teach it.

## 2. The stance (white)

Headline: There are no shortcuts in the world of authentic eroticism. (italic: authentic eroticism.)

Everywhere you look, someone is selling a quick fix these days. Three tricks for better sex. One weekend to a whole new you. More intensity, faster, louder, again.

Real erotic depth takes time, practice and a bit of courage. It gets built the way anything real gets built: brick by brick.

So here, everyone walks the whole way. Brand new to this? We take our time. Already seasoned? You'll move through the groundwork faster and you'll still walk it, because the ground is what holds you once things get intense. Nobody starts in the deep end.

If that sounds like a lot, you're reading it right. If it sounds like a relief, this is exactly for you.


## 3. How I work (black)

Headline: There's a method. There's also you. (italic: There's also you.)

The method I teach is called Connecting Heart and Sex. Six steps, held by four things that run underneath everything: presence, permission, consent and truth.

Every session has the same shape. We open: you arrive, drop into your body and we make our agreements about what's part of today. We move, at your pace. And we close properly, so whatever happened can land and come home with you.

## 5. Journey one: Connecting heart and sex (white)

Label: journey one · 10 hours

Headline: Connecting heart and sex. (italic: and sex.)

All six steps, Tend included, usually over two long days of five hours each. This is where things actually change.

01 Slow. We slow way down. Slower than feels comfortable (that's kind of the point).

You maybe hear "slow" and think boring. Hear me out. Most of us go fast because fast keeps our feelings at a safe distance. And most of us were never taught how to slow down. Arousal is a strong force and nobody showed us how to stay with it instead of rushing to chase the high. So we breathe together. We look at each other for longer than most people do. We touch at a fraction of the usual speed. Your body settles and a settled body can actually receive. Slow builds anticipation. And anticipation is delicious. It doesn't always stay slow, by the way. From here, a lot of passion can be unleashed if that feels true.

02 Feel. You notice what's actually there.

The nerves. The longing. The tenderness you've been skipping. The flicker of arousal, the tension in your jaw, maybe a little numbness. All of it is welcome here. You don't have to do anything with it yet. Just feel it. You'll start to recognise your body's yes, its no and its maybe. Your body has opinions and we listen. And if you're here with a partner, you start feeling them too. Their breath. Their tension. The exact moment they soften.

03 Speak. You name it.

Sometimes that's a pattern: "When you do this, I feel this." Sometimes it's the hot stuff: what turns you on, what you've never dared to ask for. And sometimes it's one word in the moment. "Slower." "Yes." "There." Unspoken stuff makes a room heavy and/or it keeps us questioning. Once it's named, you can actually reach each other. Listening is half of it. We’re not afraid of the usually unspoken stuff. If it turns into frustration or a fight? Great, honestly. We work with that too. Usually it shows us exactly why things haven't been sexy lately.

04 Meet. You get seen, exactly as you are.

And you stay. (Harder than it sounds. Delicious too.) Sometimes we find our way back to each other quietly. Sometimes it gets more active. Permission to be soft. Permission to be ferocious. And everything in between and beyond.

05 Flow. And then the erotic current usually unleashes on its own.

Nobody has to force it. Nobody has to perform it. It arrives as a natural consequence of the heart-y state we put you in. It moves through your belly, your pelvis, your skin, in places you forgot could feel anything. We start soft. What happens after that? Well… we'll know when we're in the room together, because every imprint is unique and so is every erotic unfolding. (Some things are better felt than read. And to keep things clear, there are strong agreements in place.) The part almost nobody has practised: actually staying while it gets hot. Most of us leave right when it gets good. Eyes close, the head takes over, fantasy, performance. Here you learn to stay. Right here.

06 Tend. Practise it until it's yours.

Tending starts right here in the room. In every session we come back to what came alive, practise it together and let it settle in your body, so it's already becoming yours before you leave. Then it goes home with you: alone or together, for years. Every session ends with something to take home and trust me, it's the kind of practice you'll actually want to do.

Just you? Every step works for one. I'm your witness and your guide and I work with you hands-on. Your own body is the other half of the conversation. (And you get to take all of it into every encounter that comes after. Lucky them.)

What you leave with: a language for what you want and what's in the way, a way to yourself or to each other, the felt experience of staying present while the current builds and a practice that's yours.

Label: journey two · 20 more hours

Headline: Erotic craft. (italic: craft.)

So your heart and sex are connected. Now what?

Once Connecting heart and sex has done its work to build a groundwork, we go deeper into the tending.

Erotic craft is where the skills live. The experience grows out of the practice. (Craft takes repetition. Lucky you.) Twenty more hours after Connecting heart and sex: four long days, six skills.

Energy. Breath and erotic energy. Building arousal, moving it through your whole body, riding it and letting it grow instead of racing to the finish.

Touch. Touch that listens as much as it gives. Asking for exactly what you want and receiving it fully (harder than it sounds for most of us). Sensual and erotic massage, taught properly.

Range. Soft and wild, tender and bold, power and surrender, edge and intensity. So many more ways to be erotic than the script most of us were handed.

Play. Curiosity, imagination and a sense of humour. Permission to experiment, get it a bit wrong and laugh about it.

Pacing. Building and resting, slow and fast, rising and settling. Choosing your speed instead of running on one.

Practice. Making it part of your actual life. How often, alone and together, small rituals and how intimacy changes with the seasons of a relationship.
