## 2. The idea (white)

Headline: You want passionate, mind-blowing, leaves-you-a-little-altered intimate life and sex. Who doesn't? (italic: Who doesn't?)

Here's what usually happens instead.

In the beginning, it's easy. The butterfly phase carries you. You can't keep your hands off each other and everything feels new and exciting because it is. It might not even feel like your absolute fuckyes but you’re willing to look past that because… well, the butterflies.

Then life settles. And somewhere along the way, your sex life settles too. Same order, same moves, same outcome. It's a little bit beige and you don’t quite know what to do about it.

Single? You know this one from the inside. You're in your head, a little bit performing, low on confidence, low on ideas. Where's your authentic sex appeal, baby? Where's your body in all this? Where's the juice?

You're stuck in a boring pattern that no longer turns you on. I call it the flatness and it's what I see most, by far.

So people reach for more. More intensity. Opening relationships. The next kink party. Fun, sometimes. And then the flatness comes back, because it was never about needing more.

What changes it is depth and aligned inspiration. Discovering that the small world you already have can hold a whole universe of eroticism. Same bed, same body, maybe even the same person you’re with and suddenly there's a door at the back you've never opened. The tiny realm of your sheets, turned into your very own Narnia. You may never want to leave.

And it doesn't stay in the bedroom. It becomes a current that's alive in how you move, eat, flirt, look at people and live out your purpose.

That kind of connection rarely just happens. It takes a little know-how. Knowledge and skill, basically. Both can be learned.

Connecting Heart and Sex is one way there. I built that method through experimentation, my own experience and a vast field study with over 2,000 people in my spaces. It's how I live my own best sex life and that's why I teach it.

I've been in a lot of rooms. From Tao tantric trainings in Asian jungles, where a well-known master told me to breathe into my anus day in and day out (yes, really), to the underworld of kink and fast sex that is Berlin.

I trained as a sex therapist and a relationship therapist. I trained in tantra and I trained in kink. I work as a sex educator and I'm an erotic ritualist at heart and in my soul. My toolbox is big and so is my lens: I look at your unique imprint first and choose from the toolbox from there.

A few of the rooms that shaped my work today:

In the therapy room, I saw how many sex lives go quiet and why. Resentment, fear, the things that never got said.

At sex parties, I saw people go fast, perform and go home with a quick fix but emptier than they arrived.

In my own temple spaces I saw it again and again and again: when people go through the heart first, something real unleashes. An erotic current, alive and authentic, that nobody has to force. And still, it doesn't always stick.

And in the spiritual bubbles I saw how often our humanity gets skipped. The messy, tender, very human parts, bypassed on the way to something higher.

Here's what all of it taught me. Your eroticism can only reach as far as you let yourself be human. So lasting eroticism makes room for all of you: the old hurts, the shame, the parts you've kept hidden, the parts you long for and everything else you are. We meet it all and work towards making peace with it. That's how a lot more of you becomes available for pleasure.

I bring a therapist's skill into the room but I teach you through the body, the heart and the beyond.
